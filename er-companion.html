Pediatric ER Companion

Step-by-step protocols for the sick child who is not in arrest, from triage to stabilisation. Every dose is calculated from the child’s weight and stops at the pediatric maximum.

Open Pediatric ER Companion About the app Privacy

What it does

28 protocols

Status epilepticus, anaphylaxis, DKA, septic and dengue shock, snake bite, poisoning, head injury, burns, meningitis and more.

Doses for this child

Calculated from a measured weight, or one estimated from age and clearly flagged as an estimate.

Drug library

58 common ER drugs with stock strengths, draw-up volumes and concentration checks for look-alike ampoules.

Scores and calculators

Pediatric GCS, PEWS, PRAM, Westley, WHO dehydration, PECARN head injury, burns and maintenance fluids.

Hands-free

Voice commands and read-aloud, with Night, Day and Bright (sunlight) colour modes.

Works offline

Installs to the home screen. Patient details stay on the device and are cleared when the app closes.

ForEmergency and pediatric teams in the first hour of care.
Based onFollows international pediatric guidelines and the trials behind them, including AHA PALS 2020, WHO, ISPAD 2022, the Surviving Sepsis Campaign, the American Epilepsy Society, GINA, the AAP, NICE and the Brain Trauma Foundation. Each protocol lists its references.

A decision-support aid for trained clinicians, not a medical device. Verify every dose before administration.
