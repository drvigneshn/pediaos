Products

Tools for pediatric teams

Three products are live and in use; a fourth is in development. All run in the browser, install to the home screen and work offline.

Code Blue Companion Live

cbc.pediaos.com

Pediatric resuscitation decision-support, aligned with AHA PALS 2020/2025 and APLS weight estimates. Enter a weight (or estimate it from age) and every dose is calculated for you.

  • Rapid assessment — tells the team if it’s an arrest and what to do next.
  • Weight-calculated doses — arrest drugs, RSI agents, infusions and equipment sizes, with safe caps.
  • Arrest console & timers — adrenaline, reassessment and shocks cued on time, with voice read-out.
  • Code log — every action timestamped for debriefing and quality improvement.

Pediatric ER Companion

Liveer.pediaos.com

Weight-based protocols for the sick child in the emergency room — from triage to stabilisation.

  • Step-by-step protocols: status epilepticus, anaphylaxis, DKA, septic and dengue shock, snake bite, poisoning, head injury, burns and more
  • Doses calculated from weight and capped at the pediatric maximum
  • Drug library with stock strengths and draw-up volumes
  • Scores: pediatric GCS, PEWS, PRAM, Westley, WHO dehydration, PECARN
For: emergency and pediatric teams

PALS Companion

Livepals.pediaos.com

A simulated pediatric bedside monitor for resuscitation teaching — shown on a laptop or projector and controlled live from the instructor’s phone.

  • Realistic rhythms and physiology — VF, pVT, PEA, SVT, heart blocks; EtCO2 that follows CPR quality
  • 12 staged scenarios with instructor-only cues and checklists
  • Automatic debrief: time to CPR, first shock, adrenaline timing, CPR fraction
  • Exam mode for skills assessment
For: simulation trainers, PALS instructors and teams

Auscultation Simulator

An interactive auscultation simulator for learning to recognise heart and breath sounds in children.

PALS Companion and the Auscultation Simulator are for education and simulation only, not for use with real patients. “PALS” is a programme of the American Heart Association; PediaOS is independent and not affiliated with or endorsed by the AHA.

About PediaOS

Clinician-built technology for children’s health

PediaOS is a pediatric health-care technology venture based in Tamil Nadu, India. We design clinical decision-support and training software for the people who care for acutely ill children — pediatricians, emergency physicians, residents and nurses.

PediaOS began in 2026 with Code Blue Companion, built from first-hand experience of pediatric resuscitation: in the first minutes of an arrest, too much depends on memory and mental arithmetic. Our tools take that load off the team, so they can focus on the child.

Code Blue Companion won the Medshark Champion Award (Best Pitch) at the ANBAI National Conclave 2026, Bengaluru.
