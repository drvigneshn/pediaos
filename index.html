About

Clinician first, builder second

I completed my DNB in Pediatrics at Kanchi Kamakoti CHILDS Trust Hospital, Chennai. My training covered general pediatric wards and OPD, the pediatric emergency department, six months of NICU and two months of PICU, with postings in nephrology, neurology, gastroenterology, pulmonology, endocrinology, hematology-oncology and infectious diseases.

I manage sick children independently, lead resuscitations, and perform the common bedside procedures — intubation, vascular access and lumbar puncture. I have peer-reviewed publications and a keen interest in teaching.

After being part of many pediatric codes, I built Code Blue Companion — a PALS decision-support app that puts the algorithms and drug doses in one place, so the team can follow them quickly during a resuscitation.

Innovation · PediaOS

Tools for the moments that matter

PediaOS is where I build bedside tools for pediatric teams — fast under pressure, aligned with guidelines, and working offline.

Code Blue Companion Live

cbc.pediaos.com

Pediatric resuscitation decision-support, aligned with AHA PALS 2020/2025 and APLS weight estimates.

“I started building this in my final year of residency after being part of many pediatric codes.”
  • Rapid assessment — tells you if it’s an arrest and what to do next.
  • Weight-calculated doses — arrest drugs, RSI agents, infusions and equipment sizes.
  • Arrest console & timers — adrenaline, reassessment and shocks cued on time, with voice read-out.
  • Code log — every action timestamped for debriefing.
