About PediaOS

Clinician-built technology for children’s health

PediaOS is a pediatric health-care technology venture based in Tamil Nadu, India. We design clinical decision-support and training software for the people who care for acutely ill children — pediatricians, emergency physicians, residents and nurses.

PediaOS began in 2026 with Code Blue Companion, built from first-hand experience of pediatric resuscitation: in the first minutes of an arrest, too much depends on memory and mental arithmetic. Our tools take that load off the team, so they can focus on the child.

Code Blue Companion won the Medshark Champion Award (Best Pitch) at the ANBAI National Conclave 2026, Bengaluru.
