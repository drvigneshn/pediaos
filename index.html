Code Blue Companion Live

cbc.pediaos.com

Pediatric resuscitation decision-support, aligned with AHA PALS 2020/2025 and APLS weight estimates. Enter a weight (or estimate it from age) and every dose is calculated for you.

  • Rapid assessment — tells the team if it’s an arrest and what to do next.
  • Weight-calculated doses — arrest drugs, RSI agents, infusions and equipment sizes, with safe caps.
  • Arrest console & timers — adrenaline, reassessment and shocks cued on time, with voice read-out.
  • Code log — every action timestamped for debriefing and quality improvement.

PediaOS PALS Coming soon

pals.pediaos.com

A learning and training platform for Pediatric Advanced Life Support — helping clinicians learn the algorithms before the code, and helping instructors teach them.

  • PALS algorithms step by step — cardiac arrest, bradycardia, tachycardia, shock and respiratory failure
  • Interactive case scenarios and mock codes that unfold in real time
  • Self-assessment quizzes and rhythm recognition for course preparation
  • Tools for instructors to run skill stations and debrief teams
For: residents, nurses, PALS course candidates and instructors

PediaOS ER Coming soon

er.pediaos.com

A bedside companion for the pediatric emergency room — for the sick child who is not (yet) in arrest, from triage to stabilisation.

  • Rapid triage and assessment — Pediatric Assessment Triangle and early warning scores
  • Step-by-step protocols for common emergencies — status epilepticus, anaphylaxis, acute asthma, DKA, septic shock
  • Weight-based drug doses, fluids and infusions in one place
  • Built to work alongside Code Blue Companion if the child deteriorates
For: emergency and pediatric teams in the first hour of care

About PediaOS

Clinician-built technology for children’s health

PediaOS is a pediatric health-care technology venture based in Tamil Nadu, India. We design clinical decision-support and training software for the people who care for acutely ill children — pediatricians, emergency physicians, residents and nurses.

PediaOS began in 2026 with Code Blue Companion, built from first-hand experience of pediatric resuscitation: in the first minutes of an arrest, too much depends on memory and mental arithmetic. Our tools take that load off the team, so they can focus on the child.

Code Blue Companion won the Medshark Champion Award (Best Pitch) at the ANBAI National Conclave 2026, Bengaluru.
